# Twist response

Read this when a twist arrives. A twist is an official change to the
problem; each one must be answered on the portal within 6 hours. Adapting
to twists is a judging criterion and the basis of the Best Shift Adapter
award ("bent the furthest without breaking").

| Twist | Released (PT) | Response due (PT) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fri 2026-10-16 23:30 | Sat 2026-10-17 05:30 |
| 2 | Sat 2026-10-17 04:30 | Sat 2026-10-17 10:30 |
| 3 | Sat 2026-10-17 07:00 | Sat 2026-10-17 13:00 |

Times come from the official schedule; changes are announced on WhatsApp.
Use the time actually announced if it differs.

## Steps

1. Capture: copy the twist text verbatim into its section of
   [../problem/twists.md](../problem/twists.md) with the release time and
   the deadline (release + 6 h).
2. Assess: which assumptions in [../problem/brief.md](../problem/brief.md)
   and which parts of [../architecture.md](../architecture.md) it breaks or
   strengthens, and what it means for the civil use and the crisis use.
3. Decide the smallest change that answers the twist visibly in the demo.
   Prefer extending the existing build over rebuilding it; the point is to
   show the build bends without breaking. Note what is deferred.
4. Implement, test, and keep `main` demo-able.
5. Write the response for the portal (what changed, why, how it shows in
   the demo) and submit it before the deadline. Submitting the portal
   response is the user's action; draft it, do not send it.
6. Record: a change record of Type `twist` and the outcome row in
   `twists.md`.
