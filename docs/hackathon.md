## The brief

Dual-use means one technology that serves civilian life and also holds up in
a military or crisis setting:

- Civil use: it earns its place on an ordinary day in LA (a port moving
  containers, a grid balancing load, a dispatcher taking routine calls).
- Crisis or defense use: the same build, unchanged, in a disaster or a
  contested environment (the fire jumps the ridge, the lines jam, the power
  is out, the convoy still has to move).

Holding only one of the two is not dual-use. Software or hardware both
count; judges score what runs on Sunday. One problem is revealed to everyone
on Friday at 18:00; nobody starts with a pre-built project.

## Judging

Five criteria, 1 to 5 points each; dual-use fit breaks ties.

1. Dual-use fit
2. Technical execution
3. Feasibility and impact
4. Adapting to twists
5. Demo and pitch

Round 1 (Sat night): a review committee scores every Devpost project and
names seven finalists. Round 2 (Sun 13:15): finalists pitch; on the demo
floor every judge counts equally, at the finals the three questioning judges
carry more weight.
